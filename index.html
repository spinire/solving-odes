Solving ODEs Numerically

Five short modules on how a computer solves a differential equation, step by step. Pictures first, a little practice, and a quick check at the end of each.

How it works

  • Each module takes 25 to 30 minutes: a reason to care, a few short lessons with interactive explorers, practice, and a five-question check. About two and a half hours in all.
  • Go in order. You need to know that a derivative is a slope; everything else is explained as it comes up.
  • Pass all five checks to get a certificate. Checks can be retried as often as you like.
